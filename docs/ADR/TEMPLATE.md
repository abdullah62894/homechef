# ADR template

Use this format for every architecture decision record.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated / superseded by [ADR-XXXX]

## Problem

What problem are we solving? Why does it matter now?

## Decision

What did we decide to do?

## Reason

Why this choice over the alternatives?

## Alternatives

What else was considered?

## Trade-offs

What did we give up or accept as a consequence?
